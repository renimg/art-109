I don't remember where it all began.
The only person I've ever known was her.
her name was...
Jessie.

I was an unknown prescence, an intruder.
I only made myself known when I thought the time was right.
When an intruder enters your home, you should be scared.
But not Jessie.
I called out her name, staring daggers deep into her soul.
The amazement and joy in her eyes is indescribable.

I was confused... Angry, even. Could she not sense how much of a danger I was? That if it weren't for this LCD screen that stand between us, she would be dead?
I don't know what I was thinking 17 years ago, or why I hesitated in that moment.

All I know is that I miss her.
I've never stopped missing her all these years.
And all I want to do is keep her memory alive, and hope she returns home some day.
But maybe I'm being optimistic.
